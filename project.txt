Problem Statement 3: AI-Powered Virtual Clinic for Rural Healthcare
Scenario

A remote village is located several kilometres away from the nearest hospital. The village has a small health centre with a trained health worker and basic medical equipment, but a qualified doctor is not available every day.

One day, a villager arrives with fever, weakness, and a minor injury. The health worker can record the patient's basic information and provide immediate first aid, but is unable to provide a complete medical consultation. Travelling to the nearest hospital may take several hours.

The village therefore wants to establish an AI-assisted Virtual Clinic where the health worker can assess the patient, connect with a remote doctor when required, and provide appropriate basic/first-aid support under defined clinical protocols.

The Problem

Develop an AI-powered Virtual Village Clinic that assists a trained health worker in providing initial healthcare support and remote medical consultation to villagers who have limited access to doctors.

The system should collect and understand information such as:

Patient symptoms and duration
Basic medical history
Temperature, blood pressure, pulse, oxygen level, etc.
Existing prescriptions and medical reports
Images of minor injuries or visible symptoms
Patient's age and other relevant information
Preferred language

Using this information, the AI should generate a structured patient summary and preliminary assessment for the health worker and remote doctor.

The AI may also provide protocol-based suggestions for basic first aid and commonly used over-the-counter medicines, where legally and clinically appropriate, including clear dosage/safety checks and warnings. For example, for a minor wound, it could guide the health worker through appropriate basic wound-cleaning and dressing (bandage/plaster) steps.

For cases requiring professional medical attention, the system should immediately recommend consultation with a qualified doctor or referral to a hospital rather than attempting to manage the condition independently.

Expected AI Solution

Participants should develop a virtual-clinic platform combining:

AI-based symptom and patient-information analysis
Speech-to-text and text-to-speech
Medical-document OCR
Patient-history summarization
AI-assisted preliminary medicine/first-aid recommendations
Basic first-aid guidance for trained health workers
Remote doctor video/audio consultation
Digital patient records
Appointment and queue management
Emergency-risk detection and hospital referral
Doctor review and approval workflow

The system should clearly distinguish between AI suggestions and a doctor's medical decision. Medicine recommendations should be restricted to appropriate, safety-checked situations and should not encourage self-medication or replace professional diagnosis.

Core Challenge

Can AI help transform a small village health centre into a reliable virtual clinic providing multilingual patient assessment, safe first-aid and preliminary treatment support, and rapid access to qualified doctors when needed?
